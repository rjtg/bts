# Screens and interaction design

The Station Office is the player's home. Open work through a room prop, bring
its file or instrument forward, and return to the same room. The desk directory
offers the same actions as a keyboard and touch alternative. See the [room and
prop map](station-office.md), [widget guide](office-widget-style.md) and [design
guidelines](design-guidelines.md).

## Workspaces by player decision

| Decision | Entry and work surface | Evidence and feedback |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Pick a programme | Shelf → format branches → tape collection or series case → taupe Tape file | Runtime on the cassette. Qualities, credits, reception and cumulative returns on the papers. Scheduling opens an editable proposal. |
| Choose or revise airtime | Desk calendar → Programming. Day is the exact-minute timeline; Week and Month summarise the playable calendar. | Selected interval, programme and ads, clashes and protected commitments. Show the station's lane and comparisons with available rival information. |
| Sell or review an airing | Timeline, Today strip, notebook reminder, or Reviews → teal file for the exact airing | Dated outlook where available, minute inventory and offer slips. Signed terms and actual provisional turnout, settlement and cost stay with the airing. |
| Develop, prepare or shoot | Script file → Ideas, Scripts or Production → collection → project spread | Standing brief or ambition, delivery, people, studio and terms. A film's pink writing and blue production papers keep the same project identity. |
| Acquire or resell a work | Incoming portfolio → illustrated Market file and industry wire | Public evidence, price and deadline. An Insight assessment may change the decision. Bought rights put a tape in the library. |
| Understand an audience | Research binder → survey orders or illustrated group file | Dated survey, half-star preferences and uncertainty. Change the shared polling schedule or investigate one group. |
| Check priorities and commitments | Notebook, ledger, review sheets, or station files | Gap and ad reminders, Sway opportunities, season goals, cash movements, broadcast receipts and local saves. |

## Player-facing interaction direction

- **Work first.** Select a title or episode, read its file and choose Next Gap,
  Recommended Slot or Choose on Planner. A supported recurring work can
  propose a regular run. Check each date before confirming.
- **Time first.** Select a gap in Programming and choose a programme. Return
  from the collection to the same gap with an editable runtime and ad-load
  preview. Use dragging with an equivalent button or picker path. A conflicting
  move leaves the previous airing in place.
- **Today stays light.** The strip follows the player's channel, accepts a tape
  drop and opens an airing on selection. Use Programming for rearrangement,
  a rival comparison and recurring changes. Clock, On Air, Up Next and the
  inspected airing answer different questions.
- **Preparation and Production share an entry and project.** The canister
  selects a stage or status. The box holds named projects grouped by show.
  Read or edit in the dossier. Choose a person from the photo spot on a role
  page and a studio from illustrated cards on the booking page. Film booking
  shows its live Rust quote, then commits once.
- **Read reports where they matter.** The Tape file is about the work over its
  life. The Airing file is about one showing, and the Ledger records station
  resources. A forecast, signed sale and result have their own dates and
  authority. Detailed internals stay in developer evidence.

## Playful feedback and visible passage of time

The visible clock, Today marker, dated industry wire, ready notices and
ledger entries give a change somewhere to land. A notice should lead to
the paper where the player can act. Manual pause brings up an OFF AIR test
card with Continue. Startup and restoration are in standby. The office
tints for its visual working hours. See [atmosphere and future animation
studies](office-playtest-direction.md) for proposals involving staff arrivals,
the exterior transition and resource-sensitive dressing.
