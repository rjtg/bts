# Daily newsroom pilot

Open **Daily newsroom** from Preparation or Production. A board creates
five consecutive daily bulletins from a standing title, reporter, anchor,
studio, air minute and ad block. The board carries each day's reporting,
recording, delivery, and transmission times. Changes to staff or studio
apply to unstarted editions. Each started edition keeps its own credits,
cost, and reservation.

Reporting starts at 09:00. At that boundary the station checks staff,
studio, airtime and cash. A clash or shortage leaves a visible missed
edition with its reason and no production charge. If the edition can
proceed, the engine commits the owned studio and airtime and spends the
reporter and anchor fees plus 100 Funds. Studio work runs from 13:00 to
13:30, and editing delivers the tape at 14:00. A 30-minute programme plus
its ad block must fit between delivery and midnight that same day.

Delivery registers a distinct nonfiction episode and turns the
reservation into a normal airing. Use the same Airing file for ads,
optional autosell and review. Same-day freshness still applies to moves
and repeats. The tape and result remain available for review after
midnight. Standard financial and ad commitment rules apply.

The first fixture has one owned studio, two reporters and two anchors.
An alternate owned studio can be assigned where one exists. Achievement
uses reporter depth and rigour, presenter recognition, and the two
participants' craft. It does not establish accuracy, political impact
or Credibility. There are no individual stories, live segments, story
deadlines, or editorial approvals in this pilot.
