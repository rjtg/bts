# Station Office playtest direction

## Working hours and atmosphere

The visual working day is 08:00–18:00. Outside it, the current office uses
cooler lighting and hides coffee steam. The clock and station controls keep
their existing rules. The desk calendar opens Programming. Wall monitors
are scenery.

Proposed art and animation for a later pass:

- Author at least two large poses per character, two medium poses within
  each large pose, and two small poses within each medium pose. A large
  change could be standing or sitting. A medium change could be picking up
  a phone. Small changes could be pen strokes or glances. Let nearby small
  frames play often enough to read as one continuous action.
- Give every character an independent cue schedule. Small changes are
  common, medium changes less frequent, and large changes rare. A new
  large pose chooses a compatible medium and small pose. Keep reduced
  motion available. Ambient cues never advance the simulation.
- Use empty-room plates and figure layers so staff can arrive between
  08:00 and 09:00 and leave between 17:30 and 18:00. Show a full steaming
  mug in the morning and an empty one in the afternoon. An optional
  exterior sequence can show departure, sunset, night, sunrise and arrival.
  It does not skip scheduled broadcasts or other simulated obligations.
- Register each wall-screen mask to its actual CRT glass. Use the same
  programme image when still or moving. Keep at least two screens showing
  motion during ordinary playback, with test cards as another source.
  Rotate programmes between screens from a shared pool of more than twelve
  short loops. Provide still frames for reduced motion.

## Playtest findings to resolve in mockups

- The Tape tray should land on the blotter in front of the furniture.
  Give it a footprint comparable to the other desk widgets and keep the
  ledger and plant outside its hit area. Explore a sorted physical rack
  with upright spines, bundled episodes and visible selection.
- Replace **Try another time** in the Tape file with an audience-appeal
  radar and two short findings, **Best aired** and **Also works**. Possible
  examples are Sunday at 13:00 and weekday evenings. Give the estimates
  their ordinary evidence and uncertainty labels.
- A **Schedule** action should carry the selected tape to Programming.
  Show feasible slots shaded orange, yellow or green for low, moderate or
  good estimated fit. Use text or a pattern as a second cue. Make conflicts
  and insufficient runtime distinct from lower appeal. Exact placement
  stays in Programming.
