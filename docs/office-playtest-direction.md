# Station Office playtest direction

## Working hours and atmosphere

The [Station Office](station-office.md#blotter-layout-and-prop-transitions)
now has an 08:00–18:00 visual working day, layered independent poses, and a shared
programme pool on the three wall CRTs. Staff appear in groups over the first
hour and leave in groups over the last half hour. Full and empty mugs indicate
the morning and afternoon. The optional exterior uses a short change of light.
Scheduling stays on the calendar and the screen reels remain decoration.

Further art can replace the small sprite movements with authored hand, pen,
and gaze frames, use figure-specific arrival paths, and stage silhouettes
walking around the exterior at sunset or sunrise. The current pose sizes
and palette are reference points for any added art.

  resource-sensitive dressing can make changes noticeable. Keep a quiet
  work surface and make notices lead to the relevant file.
