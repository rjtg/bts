# Booking desk concept

[Open the full-size concept board](controls.png).

- The recording slot is written in ink on the booking paper. Opening it
  brings up a small wire-bound desk calendar and a beige split-flap clock,
  placed as separate objects beside the dossier.
- The Preparation wheel uses the approved shallow film-can profile. Its
  raised bronze rim frames a segmented enamel lid, and the branch sticker
  sits on the cylindrical side.
- Studio cards hold clipped snapshots. The examples distinguish a compact
  set and an equipped soundstage.
- The lower outside corners are the page-turn targets. They lift when
  hovered or focused and have a visible resting cue for touch.

The physical calendar and clock are proposals for review. The canister
and studio cards have live versions based on the board. The board uses
the built-in image generator with the current
recording-clock capture and the earlier Scripts film-can study as references.
The prompt called for an 1980s cream wire-bound calendar, a beige mechanical
split-flap clock, a ruled paper time entry, bronze canister with a side-wall
sticker, clipped studio photographs, and a curled page corner.

[Studio photos](../../../web/public/office-art/studio-photos.webp) are a
separately generated contact sheet. The prompt called for a small interview
set, soundstage with overhead rigs, newsroom desk, and second station
interview set in four equal squares, with no text or UI in the art.

[Film can art](../../../web/public/office-art/film-can.webp) uses the right
panel as a reference. The built-in generator prompt asked for a transparent
sprite of an unlabelled, shallow bronze projection can with a blank cream
enamel face. The branch sticker, sectors and controls are drawn at runtime.
