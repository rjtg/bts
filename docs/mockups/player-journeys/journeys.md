# Player journeys

Current behaviour is drawn from the Chromium audit of `29f33cc`. The suggested
paths below are for review. The design work leaves the playable routes intact.

## 1. Find a tape and place it

**Current.** Shelf → canister → series case → Neighbourhood Laughs, Episode 1.
The dossier can propose the next gap or open Programming. Calendar → exact
time → Choose programme is also playable. The placement slip makes runtime
and ad minutes explicit. On touch, an overlap leaves the first airing intact
but the player has to find the conflict and a usable alternative in the slip.

**Proposed.** Pull the episode from a labelled rack and read Reception.
See the date of the available survey and compare **Best aired** with **Also
works**. Send the tape to the calendar with **Find airtime**, or start with
an exact gap and carry that date/time through the rack. Compare plausible
openings, choose one, then review content minutes, ad minutes, end time and
the affected channel on the slip. **Place airing** commits once and leaves a
selected block. The new block can open its Airing file.

- **Back or cancel** returns to the tape or original calendar gap with the
  candidate intact. Nothing is placed.
- **Blocked** names the overlapping work and time, keeps the tape in hand and
  directs the player to another time. Poor estimated fit leaves a feasible
  opening usable.
- **Complete** shows a booked block and its ad inventory. It remains distinct
  from On Air and Up Next.
- **Mechanics proposal.** Review how daypart advice is inferred from dated
  survey evidence. The design shows qualitative fit and leaves missing
  evidence unranked. It implies neither a calibrated slot forecast nor a
  contract for that audience.

## 2. Appraise and acquire rights

**Current.** The incoming portfolio opens a title index and Market dossier
over the room. The Corner Shop has price, deadline, assessment and purchase
actions. After acquisition a tape is available. A future airing blocks an
eligible resale, and the file can take the player to the affected airing.

**Proposed.** Open the selected title's offer before deciding whether more
evidence is worth Insight or whether to buy now. Keep the fixed asking price,
deadline, station Funds and evidence date in view while reading an assessment.
A ready assessment shows what changed. A signed acquisition opens **Pull tape**
with the title selected. A resale follows the same title record and shows the
exact blocking airing or the cash receipt.

- **Back or cancel** returns to the same title index. Closing an assessment
  draft before commission spends nothing.
- **Blocked** preserves ownership and shows the affected airing or expired
  offer. Offer terms can change only through the existing market rules.
- **Complete** stamps the title **On your shelf** and links to the new tape.

## 3. Decide what the station needs to learn

**Current.** The research binder separates Survey orders from audience
dossiers. Young viewers has a dated snapshot. Turning away from an unsubmitted
Insight setting and back keeps that draft. One polling schedule covers all
groups.

**Proposed.** Open Young viewers and read the pinned survey before deciding
whether the real problem is stale evidence or uncertain evidence. **Survey
orders** shows next poll and fees. **Investigate this group** shows the chosen
Insight and remaining balance. Confirm spending once. Stamp the investigation
with when improved estimates can become visible. Keep the current paper dated
until a successful poll.

- **Back or cancel** leaves the selected survey and the uncommitted amount
  available. An unaffordable spend explains the balance.
- **Complete** leaves a dated investigation receipt and the current survey,
  even if polling is off. Show how to arrange the next survey in that case.

## 4. Find the next production decision

**Current.** The script prop opens the Ideas, Scripts and Production branches.
Harbour Lights is grouped in a file box. Individual scripts carry their own
status and a booked film follows the assigned cast and studio. Discussion,
hiring and newsroom remain workbook sheets. The player can return to the room
and resume a film page.

**Proposed.** Open a stage, pick the show folder, and see only editions with
work in that state. Show a date and next action on each relevant entry. Opening
a script goes to the exact episode's page. Acceptance leads directly to the
same episode's casting and booking leaves. Discussion and newsroom runs reuse
the show folder and a future-edition work schedule. Changes list affected
dates and keep earlier editions and signed terms intact.

- **Back or cancel** returns to the same collection and tab, preserving draft
  photos and dates. Signed fees or work orders are visible commitments.
- **Blocked** names the unavailable person, room or edition and its dates.
  Choose again on the same paper.
- **Complete** stamps the booking, then a delivery notice opens the correct
  tape. Script purchase, script resale and broader production lifecycle
  changes belong to separate design work.

## 5. Understand an airing and follow its money

**Current.** An airing file shows minute offers and can preserve a pre-air
outlook viewed in the session. After transmission it contains turnout and
settlement. The Reviews prop opens a log with a selected Airing file.
Finances still opens a separate worksheet over the office.

**Proposed.** Open the filed showing from Today, Programming or Reviews.
Face the pre-air slip against the transmission receipt. Show **No outlook
filed** if no dated outlook exists. Put provisional turnout, aired ad
minutes, settled cash and airing cost in aligned rows. Follow the entry into
the Ledger for related transactions, or follow the programme into the Tape
file for lifetime receipts and production spending.

- **Back** from the ledger or Tape file returns to this airing and the
  originating log position. Opening a result changes no terms.
- **Blocked or exceptional** distinguishes off-air escrow release, pending
  settlement and zero viewers. A payable minute sale need not meet a
  particular audience target.
- **Complete** leaves a dated receipt and an optional choice to plan another
  showing. Ratings, signed terms and station returns have their own meanings.
  There is no composite success score.

Keep station goals, reminders and timed Sway requests reachable from their
current entry while #4 is deferred. Keep a focusable directory route during
any later change to the transport under #1.
