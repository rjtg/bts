# Two visual directions

Open the [exploration board](index.html#directions) for both treatments.

| Decision | Broadcast control room | Producer’s office |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Timeline | Wide timing board, aligned station lanes and a shared playhead. This makes collision checks visible. | Printed day sheet with movable notes. Easy to read as an agenda, with weaker duration and rival comparison. |
| Clock | Persistent amber timecode, transport and red pilot lamp. | Wall clock and Today tray create atmosphere, but may duplicate precise times. |
| Navigation | Source tabs open desks. Drawers carry the selected object. | Physical folders make the workspace memorable, but switching tasks often means leaving the scene. |
| Commercial decisions | Exact terms fit beside the segment strip; amber keys identify the next commitment. | Stamped offer cards make deals feel tangible, but layering them hides comparisons and focus order. |

Use the **broadcast control room** for the shell and timing board. Borrow paper
dossiers, portrait contact sheets, receipts and the research dial from the
office. Keep the instrument strip legible across all desks. A warm sunset light
behind the masthead can change with the simulated hour while text contrast
holds steady. Pause holds the lighting still. Reduced motion uses instant
changes between static light states.
