# Blotter office browser evidence

The clean reference establishes where a job lands. The denser reference
supplies the lamp, paperwork, and other edge dressing. The reference images
are from `origin/main` at `f53ee81` and shown at the desktop capture width.

## Replay

1. Start in the office at 1280 × 900. The notebook rests on the blotter.
2. Hover the Research binder, then press Enter or click it. Watch the binder
   move to the middle before the existing research workspace opens.
3. Change the Insight slider without spending. Close the workspace with
   Escape and reopen it. The draft stays set and focus returns to the binder.
4. Open the notebook and close it. The studio crews change pose at idle.
   Open the clipboard for Film
   Production and use Back to Office to return.
5. Repeat at 390 × 844 with touch and reduced motion. The scenes open
   immediately and the room stays visible around the focused workspace.

From the built `web/` preview:

```sh
BTS_BLOTTER_CAPTURES=../contrib/blotter-office/after npm run test:e2e
```

The test pauses browser animations at explicit positions for the short
captures. The gallery uses the first five samples of the 280 ms binder
movement and a sampled sequence of the persistent idle poses. The wider test suite
covers exact-minute scheduling, protected ad terms, booking and settlement.

## Comparison

The baseline shows loose props across an uninterrupted wood tabletop. The
blotter pass groups the ledger with receipts and calculator, puts
preparation by the lamp, and shares the right edge with the phone and
calendar. The first Chromium pass exposed the bottom edge grazing the
viewport. The final desktop height follows the space below transport so the
blotter and notebook fit. The narrow layout stacks prop controls beside a
scrollable workspace. Dense forms retain their existing presentation in
an overlay. The ambient loops are small sprite motions independent of
the simulation clock.

The idle-motion clip samples browser poses at each 1.4-second selection
with the station clock stopped. The capture uses a repeatable random sequence. The steam uses separate moving wisps, the foliage
moves independently of its pot, and the staff member changes to a glance
pose and back.
